Open the enrollment QR or link shared by your teacher. Complete the guided camera form, then track your approval on that same link.

  1. Confirm your student name and roll number.
  2. Give consent and follow the blink and head-turn instructions.
  3. Capture front, left and right views, then submit.
  4. Your teacher approves your profile before face attendance is available.

Use your own phone camera. Photos and videos are not uploaded; only a numeric face profile is saved. If camera enrollment is unavailable, ask your teacher to use QR attendance.
