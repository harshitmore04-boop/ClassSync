Your attendance outlook

Assumes every future class increases the total by one. Results use the exact percentage before rounding.

How the calculation works

Current attendance = attended ÷ total × 100.

Below target: find the smallest whole number x such that (attended + x) ÷ (total + x) reaches the target. Above target: find the largest whole number m such that attended ÷ (total + m) stays at the target.

For 34 out of 45 at a 75% target, attendance is 75.56%. Missing the next class gives 34/46 = 73.91%, so there are currently zero safe misses.

What happens if a check fails?

The system rejects attendance and shows a reason. An unknown identity requires registration; an expired QR requires a fresh session; a duplicate does not create another record.
